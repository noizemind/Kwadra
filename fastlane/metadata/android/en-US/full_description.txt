Kwadra is an 8×8 block puzzle. Three pieces wait in the tray. Drag one onto the board. A full row or a full column vanishes. Clearing several lines at once raises the combo, and emptying the whole board adds a bonus.

The pieces take their hue from the phone accent. Choose system, light, or dark. The interface is in Russian, Ukrainian, English, Belarusian, Estonian, Japanese, French, German, Chinese, Korean, and Vietnamese.

Sound and vibration follow the place, the clear, and a rejected drop. Both can be switched off. Nothing is sent off the phone: there is no account, no ad, and no network request. An unfinished game stays on the device.
